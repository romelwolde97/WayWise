Privacy Policy for WayWise

Effective Date: November 2026

WayWise is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

1. Location Data (GPS)

WayWise requests access to your device's precise location (GPS) strictly while the app is in use to display nearby transit stops, taxis, and routes. We do not store or sell your location history.

2. Accessibility Features

Text-to-Speech and vibration alerts run locally on your device without external data collection.
